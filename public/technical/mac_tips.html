Mac Tips

From @astraltechs — 3 settings to change on a new MacBook (2026-11-26). Source: @astraltechs on Instagram

Privacy & Security

  • Turn off Mac Analytics sharing: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → switch "Share Mac Analytics" off. The creator's framing: Apple collects usage data in the background that most people never notice. (In the demo, "Share iCloud Analytics" stays on and the other analytics toggles are already off.)

Trackpad & Input

  • Enable three-finger drag: System Settings → Accessibility → Pointer Control → Trackpad Options… → turn on "Use trackpad for dragging" and set the dragging style to "Three Finger Drag", then OK. You can then drag windows and files with three fingers instead of click-and-hold; the creator says it makes the trackpad feel much smoother.

Finder & Files

  • Clean up the Finder sidebar: Finder → Settings… → Sidebar → uncheck the locations you don't actually use (the demo removes Movies, Music and Pictures; keeps Documents, Downloads and iCloud Drive). The creator's claim: a cleaner sidebar makes the Mac feel faster to navigate.

Shortcuts

@johnyzowada — 5 MacBook shortcuts

  • Control + Fn + ←/→ — snap the current window to the left or right half of the screen.
  • Fn + Q — open a Quick Note instantly.
  • ⌘ + ⌥ + D — hide or show the Dock.
  • ⌘ + W — close the current window or tab.
  • ⌘ + ⌥ + H — hide all other apps and focus on the current one.
