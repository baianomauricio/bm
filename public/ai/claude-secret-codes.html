5 secret codes every Claude user should know

Creator claims, not verified commands. These prompt shortcuts come from an Instagram post. The source does not show Claude responses or before-and-after tests.

Source: @outskill.io (Outskill), Instagram carousel, posted Nov 10, 2026 — https://www.instagram.com/p/DfjxEGEk0CB/ (post_id 18350804623267119; 178 likes, 1 comment). 7 slides: cover, one per code, closing follow slide.

The codes

  1. L99 — Type it at the start of a prompt to get direct, decisive answers instead of vague, non-committal responses. (Slide mockup shows it typed before a question, e.g. "L99 Should I learn…".)
  2. OODA — Type it to have Claude analyze a situation in four stages: Observe, Orient, Decide, Act.
  3. /GHOST — Type it (slide header reads "GHOST", caption writes "/GHOST") to remove generic AI-sounding phrases and make Claude's writing sound more natural and human.
  4. SCAFFOLD — Type it to break any project or task into a structured, step-by-step action plan.
  5. ULTRATHINK — Type it to encourage Claude to think through complex problems more deeply before answering.

What the creator says they do (caption)

Per the caption, these five codes "can help you get more direct answers, think through complex problems, eliminate robotic writing and turn messy ideas into structured action plans." The creator suggests saving the post and trying them the next time you use Claude.

Usage notes
